# Virginia data centers, power and the 2026 Energy Plan

Run from this folder with `TECHGRAPH_BACKEND=scipy`.

## Models
- `tg_case9.py`: version 9, the case model under its own assumptions (2025-2050, representative days with a 72-hour
  cold snap, institutions, workload-based data-center flexibility, RGGI, a net-zero cap). Reports total cost and resource
  cost (excluding RGGI payments, carbon charges on imports and compliance payments).
- `tg_case10.py`: version 10, the Energy Plan's pathways under the plan's own assumptions (Appendix C, Tables 22-26):
  plan technology costs and dates, nuclear limits and the 400 MW fusion plant, transmission growth to 20 GW, the 30%
  import reliance cap, capacity-import caps, solar and offshore wind limits, the Virginia RGGI allowance trajectory with
  in-state accounting (Mt Storm counted as an import), and the plan's pathway definitions (`plan_case`). The RGGI cap is
  strict by default; `rggi_escape=60` lets the zone buy allowances from other RGGI states.
- `va_days2.json`: the eight representative days. `va_profiles2.py` rebuilt them from raw hourly Dominion load and GenX
  variability data, which are not included.

## Experiment sets
- `runs9.py` to `runs12.py` (version 9): pathways; leakage and rates; compute policies by workload mix; reliability stress
  tests. `article_numbers.py` derives every cited number (`results/numbers.json`); `figs_fix.py` draws the figures.
- `runs20.py` to `runs22.py` (version 10): the same under the plan's assumptions. `plan_numbers.py` derives
  `results/plan_numbers.json`.
- Run outputs (`runs*.json`, 3-5 MB each) are regenerated by the scripts and are not stored.

## Regression check
`python3 check_reference.py` reruns two cases that exercise every rule type and compares them with
`results/reference_runs.json`. It must print PASS (zero difference).
