# Cases

Applications of Techgraph to real geographies and policy regimes. Each case imports the package from the repository
root and runs from its own folder.

- `virginia_dc/`: data-center electricity in the PJM Dominion zone, the 2026 Virginia Energy Plan pathways, and the
  plan-aligned replication.
- `potomac_water/`: the Washington metropolitan area water supply system, a replication of ICPRB's 2025 water supply
  study, and the coupled data-center, power and water analysis.
